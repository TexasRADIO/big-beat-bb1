# BB1 for FM-1

**An ode to drum machines.**

Two drum layers. Dillo on bass. A full effects chain — delay, reverb, seven distortions, a compressor. Pick a kit, find a pattern and play. Then see how far you can push it.

BB1 grew out of the collection and research behind [Drum Machines Have No Soul](https://drummachineshavenosoul.com/). We have the machines. We care about the machines. That is why we built this.

## Inside the box

| Part | What you play |
| --- | --- |
| Big Beat A + B | Two drum layers, each with its own kit, pattern and level |
| Dillo | Bass sounds and phrases; kick-aware bass response in 2.3 |
| Effects chain | Delay (DIGI/TAPE), room reverb, seven distortions, glue compressor |
| Studio | A level, B level, Dillo level and FX return on four labelled knobs |

Four kit choices: **native 808 synthesis**, plus selected **LinnDrum**, **DrumTraks** and **DMX** sample sets. Two layers play together. See the [compatibility notes](docs/KITS.md) for substitutions and differences from the original machines.

The catalogue contains **67 song titles / 295 song sections**, **138 Roland presets on four shelves**, and **168 era sections across 21 eras**: **601 sections in total**. These are catalogue references, not recordings or artist endorsements.

The browser emulator is an older r9 preview with 21 song titles. It does not load the sampled-kit library, reproduce exact panel layout/key LEDs, or model hardware timing and CPU load. Check the version you are playing.
