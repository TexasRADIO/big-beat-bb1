# Kits and compatibility

The r9.8 candidate offers native 808 synthesis and selected LinnDrum, DrumTraks and DMX sample kits. These are four choices per drum layer, with two layers playing together. A selected sound set does not reproduce every voice, circuit or feature of its source machine.

The pattern shelves and kit choices are separate. The CR-8000, CR-78, TR-66 and TR-77 Roland shelves contain preset patterns; they are not additional sound engines.

## Declared substitutions

Playback routing preserves the source event while choosing an available voice. The PATTERN screen can show contextual SUBST feedback for the intended kit/pattern. In native 808 on drum B, synth percussion maps to cowbell and zap maps to high tom instead of using the shared auxiliary bank.

The expanded r9.8 source catalogue declares three additional translations:

| Source voice | Played voice | Song |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TR-727 short whistle | High conga | Acid Tracks |
| Quijada | Claves | Oxygène IV |
| Deep drum | Low tom | Sweet Dreams |

These are source/host mappings, not claims of an authentic extra voice or listening acceptance. The qualified source release must include the full exact-version routing inventory and remaining notices.
